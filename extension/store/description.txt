Kailah Wallet is a self-custody crypto wallet that runs in your browser. One 12-word recovery phrase holds your coins on nine networks, including Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana and TRON, along with the USDC and USDT stablecoins. Your keys are created and encrypted on your device and never leave it.

EVERY TRANSACTION IS CHECKED BEFORE SIGNING
• TRON: the network node builds the transaction, and the app decodes it field by field. Anything it didn't ask for blocks signing.
• Solana swaps (through Jupiter): the instructions must pass an allow-list, and an on-chain simulation must match the quote.
• Ethereum-style networks: no token approvals, ever. Every signature names its chain.
• A double-pay guard blocks a new send while an earlier one can't be confirmed.
• A lookalike-address warning stops a send to an address that starts and ends like one you've paid but isn't it, the "address poisoning" scam.
• Swaps can only move the two coins on screen, never your other tokens.
• Ledger (Bitcoin and Litecoin): only signatures come back from the device, each checked against the app's own copy of the transaction. New; tested against an emulator so far, so start with small amounts.

PRACTICE MODE
One switch moves everything to test networks, with faucet links built in. Send and receive worthless coins until you're comfortable.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No accounts, analytics, cookies, ads or trackers. No server of ours sees your balances.
• The app can contact only a fixed list of public blockchain and price services, and the browser enforces that list.
• Your recovery phrase is encrypted with AES-256-GCM under a key derived with Argon2id. Cached data is encrypted too, and everything locks after 15 minutes idle.

ALSO
• Staking on Solana and TRON; swaps between SOL and USDC (a 0.5% Kailah fee, shown before you confirm)
• QR code scanning, payment alerts (with no amounts) and CSV export
• Face ID or fingerprint unlock with a passkey, where your device supports it (your password always works)
• Show only the networks you use; hidden ones are never queried

REFERRAL LINKS
The Buy screens list exchanges where you can buy coins. Three of them, Strike, Kraken and Gemini, are the developer's referral links, labelled "Invite link" in the app. A new account made through one gets that exchange's current sign-up offer under its own terms (fee-free trading up to a set amount on Strike, a sign-up bonus on Kraken and Gemini; offers vary by country), and the developer receives a referral reward. They open only when you tap them, and referral codes are never added to other links or to sites you visit.

PLEASE NOTE
Kailah is maintained by one person. It has had AI-assisted security reviews, not a third-party audit, so keep amounts small. Nobody can recover a lost recovery phrase or reverse a send: write your 12 words on paper and keep them safe.

PERMISSIONS
• Display notifications: for payment alerts.
• Unlimited storage: so the browser never evicts your encrypted keys.
It cannot read or change the websites you visit.
